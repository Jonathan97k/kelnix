01 — WHO WE ARE

A considered partner for complex technology opportunities.

KELNIX Technologies is a Malawi-based technology sourcing and digital solutions company working with organisations, software creators and asset owners. We help translate a defined requirement into a structured search, a clearer view of available evidence and a practical next conversation.

Our work is grounded in communication, permission and diligence. We aim to make it easier for buyers to describe what they need, for owners to understand an opportunity, and for both sides to decide whether a discussion should proceed.

We approach each engagement individually. Scope, access, evaluation criteria and commercial terms are clarified with the relevant parties rather than assumed in advance.

Our working principle: clarity before commitment. Every opportunity should have a defined brief, an appropriate permission path and evidence that can be reviewed.

03 — SPECIALISED FOCUS

Private software & game-development repositories.

For buyers seeking established development codebases and associated assets, KELNIX can coordinate sourcing and an initial eligibility review. This includes private projects built with Unity, Unreal Engine and other relevant technologies, subject to the buyer's brief and the owner's willingness to participate.

Private status alone does not establish ownership or transfer rights. Any repository inspection, asset disclosure, purchase or licence discussion must follow the rights holder's written authorisation and the parties' agreed confidentiality and access conditions.

04 — OUR PROCESS

A disciplined process that keeps expectations clear.

01

Define the requirement

Agree the technology scope, screening criteria, intended use, required evidence and commercial expectations.

02

Identify potential projects

Research relevant projects and make contact through appropriate channels without implying access or authority that has not been granted.

03

Confirm permission

Establish the rights holder's identity and authority, and agree confidentiality, disclosure and access conditions before restricted review.

04

Coordinate technical review

Arrange authorised review of commit history, authorship, code structure, documentation, dependencies and associated assets.

05

Document findings

Present verified observations, limitations, exceptions and unresolved questions so the buyer can make an informed assessment.

06

Support next steps

If both sides wish to proceed, coordinate discussion of valuation, permitted use, licence or purchase terms and written agreements.

05 — TRUST & RESPONSIBILITY

Trust is demonstrated through the process.

We respect the developers, owners, buyers and reviewers involved in every opportunity.

✓

Permission first

Private code and restricted materials are only requested or shared with appropriate authorisation.

✓

Ownership verification

Ownership, contributor rights, third-party components and asset permissions must be examined before any transfer or licence.

✓

Confidentiality

Sensitive project information and technical access should follow agreed confidentiality and security conditions.

✓

Evidence over assumptions

Claims are treated as unverified until supported by records, documentation or appropriate review.

✓

Clear responsibilities

Each party should understand its role, obligations, permitted use and decision authority.

✓

Owner control

Rights holders remain free to decline, limit disclosure or negotiate their own terms.
