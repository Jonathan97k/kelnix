WHAT TO INCLUDE

A useful brief makes sourcing more precise.

PROJECT SCOPE

Engine and project type

Unity, Unreal, Godot or another engine; shipped games, prototypes, tools, plugins or other software.

TECHNICAL SIGNALS

History and authorship

Commit history, merged pull requests, first-party authored code, contributors, tests, documentation and build context.

TRANSACTION

Rights and intended use

Whether you seek a licence or acquisition, intended use, required exclusivity, confidentiality and decision timeline.

Any thresholds such as commit counts, repository age, lines of code or merged pull requests are buyer-specific screening criteria—not universal quality measures or guarantees of acceptance.
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Clear roles at every stage.

01

Brief review

We clarify your must-haves, preferences, exclusions and evidence requirements.

02

Potential source identification

We identify possible projects and contact owners to determine interest and authority to engage.

03

Permission and confidentiality

We coordinate the necessary owner consent and NDA or other confidentiality process before restricted access.

04

Buyer-led technical review

Your team or an appointed independent reviewer evaluates the repository and associated materials. KELNIX can coordinate the process and organise information.

05

Commercial discussion

You and the rights holder discuss valuation, licence scope, purchase terms and final agreements.
