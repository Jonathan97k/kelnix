YOUR CONTROL & CONFIDENTIALITY

No access or deal without your agreement.

01

Initial description

Start with a high-level description. Do not send private source code, credentials, secrets or customer data in an initial enquiry.

02

Ownership and authority

We discuss who owns the project, whether contributors or clients have rights, and who can authorise a discussion.

03

Confidentiality terms

Before sharing restricted information, the relevant parties agree appropriate confidentiality and permitted-use terms.

04

Review and offer discussion

You decide what information may be shared and whether to proceed. You may decline an opportunity; no licence or transfer occurs without a signed agreement.

A repository may include third-party libraries, contractor work, client-owned code, licensed tools or other dependencies. These rights need to be understood before any proposed transfer or licence.
