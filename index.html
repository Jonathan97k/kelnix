OUR STANDARDS

Trust is not a tagline. It is a working standard.

We believe good technology sourcing respects the people who build, own and evaluate the work.

✓

Permission first

Private source code and restricted materials are only requested or shared with appropriate authorisation.

✓

Evidence over assumptions

Project claims should be supported by repository records or documentation and labelled when unverified.

✓

Confidentiality respected

Evaluation access and sensitive information should follow agreed NDA and access conditions.

✓

Owners stay in control

Rights holders decide whether to license, sell, share or decline an opportunity.
