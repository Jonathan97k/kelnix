RESPONSIBLE SOURCING

Trust is built into the process.

A repository's size or commit count is not enough. We pay attention to provenance, permission and the evidence behind every opportunity.

✓

Rights-holder authorisation

We seek permission before requesting or sharing private source code, project files or restricted materials.

✓

Evidence over assumptions

Metrics and project claims should be supported by repository records or documentation and clearly labelled when unverified.

✓

Confidentiality first

Confidential information and evaluation access should be handled under the parties' agreed NDA and access conditions.

✓

Clear commercial boundaries

Owners retain control of their assets and decide whether to license, sell, share or decline an opportunity.
