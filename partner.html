WHAT KELNIX DOES

Practical coordination across technology opportunities.

Our work starts by understanding the requirement, the people involved and the evidence needed to determine whether an opportunity is suitable.

01 / SOURCING

Technology sourcing

Identify software projects, private repositories and digital assets against defined technical and commercial requirements.

02 / OWNER ENGAGEMENT

Rights-holder coordination

Engage developers and authorised representatives to clarify ownership, availability, permissions and disclosure conditions.

03 / EVALUATION

Evidence-led evaluation

Organise repository history, development activity, documentation and authorised technical review for informed decisions.

Web and automation

Data and content operations

Commercial coordination

HOW WE WORK

A clear process from first contact to next steps.

Submitting your details does not automatically qualify an opportunity or create a contract. KELNIX reviews each enquiry before proposing a next step.

01 — Understand

We review your identity, organisation, role and reason for contacting KELNIX.

02 — Assess fit

We consider relevance, available information, authority, technical fit and delivery capacity.

03 — Verify

Where relevant, we request evidence and confirm rights or permission before progressing.

04 — Respond

We contact you using the details you provide to discuss a suitable next step.
