Privacy notice

This notice explains how KELNIX Technologies handles information submitted through the partnership portal and related enquiries.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

KELNIX Technologies (“KELNIX”, “we”, “us”) respects the privacy of people and organisations that contact us. This notice describes the information collected through our partnership registration experience and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may process your name, email address, phone number, company or organisation, country, website or professional profile, your role, service interests, expected timeline, and the message you provide. We also collect limited technical information sent by your browser with the form: browser user-agent, platform, language, time zone, screen and viewport dimensions, the page URL and referring page (if available). This technical information helps us understand form submissions and protect the service. We do not use it to uniquely identify or fingerprint your device.

2. Why we use it

  • To receive, assess and respond to business and partnership enquiries.
  • To review, assess and respond to partnership applications and business enquiries.
  • To communicate about relevant services, next steps, meetings or opportunities related to your application.
  • To maintain application records, protect the portal and address misuse or security concerns.

3. Service providers

The enquiry form uses Supabase to securely process and store submissions. If email notifications are enabled, an email delivery provider may also process the enquiry details to deliver the notification to KELNIX.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We limit access to application information to people who need it to review or manage the enquiry. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security or integrity of KELNIX, applicants or the portal.

5. Retention and deletion requests

We keep application information for as long as reasonably necessary to assess and manage the relationship, maintain appropriate business records, and address legitimate operational or legal needs. To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, email info.kelnix@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and identify the relevant application. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some records may need to be retained where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

6. Security

We use server-side validation, access controls and database permissions to protect enquiry records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, private keys, confidential source code or other sensitive materials through the form.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to submit an enquiry. You may also contact us to ask questions or request correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable obligations.

8. Children

The partnership portal is intended for business and professional enquiries, not for children.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the portal or our practices change. The updated date above indicates when the latest version was published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact info.kelnix@gmail.com.

This notice is an operational privacy explanation and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
