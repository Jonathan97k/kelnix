WHAT WE SOURCE

Development work with its history and context.

Our focus includes private repositories and associated project materials. The exact scope depends on the buyer brief, owner participation and the rights that can be established.

01 / GAME DEVELOPMENT

Unity & Unreal projects

Game codebases, prototypes, tools, plugins and related assets where owners can establish the right to discuss and license them.

02 / OTHER ENGINES

Additional game technology

Godot, custom engines and other development environments when they match the buyer’s requirements.

03 / SOFTWARE

Private software repositories

Software projects with useful development history, documentation and context, subject to ownership and licence review.

HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS

A controlled process from brief to review.

01

Understand the brief

We document engine, project type, history, code, asset and commercial requirements.

02

Identify potential projects

We research potential sources and approach relevant owners without implying access or authority.

03

Establish permission

Owners decide whether to participate and what information may be disclosed. Confidentiality terms are agreed before restricted material is shared.

04

Coordinate evaluation

We organise available evidence and help arrange technical review by the buyer or its appointed reviewer.

05

Support next steps

We coordinate discussions. Buyer and owner decide licensing, purchase terms and legal documentation.

KELNIX is a sourcing and coordination partner. We do not claim ownership of third-party projects, guarantee availability or replace the buyer’s technical, legal or commercial due diligence.
