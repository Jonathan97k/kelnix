Questions and answers

Before you get started

Simple answers to common questions about requesting a website or digital service from Kelnix.

Do I need all my text and photos before I enquire?

No. Start with your business name, what you do, and what you want to achieve. Kelnix can help organize the website structure, refine your wording, and advise on suitable images and other materials. We will agree on any content or photography requirements before work begins.

What happens after I submit the form?

The form prepares an enquiry message and opens WhatsApp. You review the message and press Send. Once it reaches Kelnix, we review your requirements, ask any necessary questions, and discuss a suitable scope, quotation, and timeline.

How much does a business website cost?

Kelnix offers a single-page business website package starting from MK 50,000. More complex websites, online stores, integrations, and custom systems are quoted according to their requirements. The final price is agreed with you before development begins.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Explain your business and what you need in everyday language. We will guide you through the technical decisions and explain the options clearly.

Can Kelnix help if I only have a business idea?

Yes. Choose “Not sure yet — help me decide” in the form and describe the problem you want to solve. We can discuss a practical starting point before you commit to a project.

Am I committed to buying after sending an enquiry?

No. An enquiry is a conversation, not a purchase. We discuss the scope and price first. Development begins only after you approve the proposal and agreed terms.

How do I pay?

Payment arrangements depend on the project and are discussed in the quotation. Do not make a payment until you have received and agreed to the project scope and payment terms.
