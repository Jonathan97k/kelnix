COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers before we connect your systems.

We assess each requirement against the tools, permissions, data and operating environment available.

Can AI search for customers and write customised messages?

Yes. A workflow can research prospects from permitted sources, qualify them against agreed criteria and prepare personalised drafts. Sending should follow your approval rules and applicable platform policies.

Can you automate email and customer enquiries?

Yes. Workflows can classify and summarise messages, draft responses, route enquiries and create follow-up tasks. The exact level of automation depends on access and risk.

Do you build fully autonomous AI employees?

We build scoped assistants and connected workflows. We do not assume unrestricted autonomy; sensitive decisions and external actions should retain appropriate human oversight.

Can you use our existing business tools?

Often, where the platform provides a suitable API or integration and the account owner grants access. We check compatibility before confirming scope.

Can we start with one small automation?

Absolutely. A focused pilot is often the best way to validate value before expanding into a larger system.
