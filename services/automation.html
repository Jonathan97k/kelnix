COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful details before we begin.

Have a requirement that is not listed? Send it through and we can assess it without assuming it is already part of the standard scope.

Can AI search for customers and write customised messages?

A workflow can discover and qualify prospects from permitted sources and prepare personalised drafts. Sending messages should follow the client's approval rules and applicable platform policies.

Can you automate email and customer enquiries?

Yes, workflows can classify, summarise and draft responses, or route enquiries to the right person. The level of automation depends on access, integrations and risk.

Do you build fully autonomous AI employees?

We focus on reliable, scoped assistants and workflows. External actions, sensitive decisions and high-impact operations should retain appropriate human oversight.

Can you connect existing business tools?

Potential integrations depend on the systems, APIs, account permissions and data policies. We assess these during discovery before promising a connection.
