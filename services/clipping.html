COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful details before we begin.

Have a requirement that is not listed? Send it through and we can assess it without assuming it is already part of the standard scope.

Can you work with podcasts and interviews?

Yes. KELNIX can repurpose long-form conversations into short clips for social platforms, based on the supplied recordings and agreed editorial direction.

Can you edit livestreams in real time?

Real-time studio editing depends on the platform, source feeds, team access, equipment and required turnaround. KELNIX can scope the workflow first; live coverage is not promised until those requirements are verified.

What can be included?

Moment selection, hook refinement, vertical editing, captions, basic audio and visual polish, branded styling, platform exports and organised delivery can be scoped.

Can you work with an existing brand style?

Yes. Provide brand colours, logo, fonts, examples and any editorial rules so the output can follow your existing identity.
