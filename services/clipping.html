GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before production starts.

We will confirm the details that affect quality, timing and cost before accepting a production schedule.

What footage can you work with?

Podcasts, interviews, webinars, recorded livestreams, event footage and other supplied video sources, subject to file access and agreed usage rights.

Can you edit livestreams in real time?

Potentially, depending on the platform, source feeds, studio access, equipment, staffing and required turnaround. We assess the setup before confirming what is achievable.

Can AI find the best moments?

Transcription and AI-assisted moment suggestions can speed up discovery. Editorial selection and final context checks remain part of the review process.

Can you publish directly to our social accounts?

That can be explored where the platform supports the required integration and you grant the appropriate access. Otherwise, we deliver approved files and publishing copy for your team.

Can we set up a recurring monthly workflow?

Yes. We can scope a recurring volume, delivery rhythm, review process and optional automation once we understand your production needs.
