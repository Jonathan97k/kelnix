THE ROLE WE PLAY

We connect a buyer's brief to a reviewable project opportunity.

KELNIX operates as a sourcing and coordination partner. We translate requirements into a practical search, communicate with developers or authorised representatives, and prepare available evidence for the buyer's own assessment. We do not claim ownership of projects or make acceptance decisions on a buyer's behalf.

ACCESS & RESPONSIBILITY

Private does not mean unrestricted.

Trust is fundamental when working with non-public code, project history and development assets. KELNIX treats access as a permissioned process, not an assumption.

  • Owner-authorised participationPrivate materials are requested only from an owner or properly authorised representative.
  • Agreed confidentialityDisclosure and review terms are established before restricted information is shared.
  • Purpose-limited evaluationAccess is scoped to the agreed project and evaluation purpose.
  • Evidence, not guaranteesWe report what can be supported and flag what remains unverified.
QUESTIONS & CLARITY

Before a sourcing engagement begins.

We prefer clear expectations over assumptions. These answers explain how the service is structured.

Discuss a specific brief ↗
Does KELNIX sell or own the repositories?

No. KELNIX supports sourcing and coordination. Ownership remains with the relevant rights holders unless a separate, properly documented transaction establishes otherwise.

Can you guarantee a repository will meet a buyer's criteria?

No. We can screen against agreed criteria and organise available evidence. Some information may be incomplete or require direct technical verification. The buyer makes the final acceptance decision.

Will you access private repositories without permission?

No. Private repository access and disclosure must be authorised by the owner or a properly authorised representative. We do not bypass access controls.

What if a project has no complete commit or pull-request history?

We report the available evidence and its limitations. Whether an incomplete history is acceptable depends on the buyer's brief and review process.

Can you source Unity or Unreal Engine projects?

Yes, those are relevant project categories for this service. Actual sourcing depends on the brief, candidate availability and the rights holder's willingness and authority to participate.

What happens after KELNIX submits a candidate?

The buyer reviews the evidence and may request further checks or an authorised technical evaluation. Any next step, access and commercial terms are agreed by the relevant parties.
