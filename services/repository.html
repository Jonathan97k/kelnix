COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful details before we begin.

Have a requirement that is not listed? Send it through and we can assess it without assuming it is already part of the standard scope.

What kind of repositories are relevant?

Private software repositories and game-development projects, especially Unity and Unreal Engine, plus associated assets where rights and permissions can be established.

What are the current screening signals?

Some briefs may look for 100+ commits, ideally 100+ merged pull requests, 5,000+ first-party authored lines of code and at least six months of development history. These are buyer-specific screening signals, not universal guarantees or proof of acceptance.

Does KELNIX need access to private code immediately?

No. Initial discussions should use high-level information. Restricted access or disclosure only happens after the rights holder authorises it and appropriate terms are agreed.

Does KELNIX guarantee acceptance?

No. KELNIX helps source, organise evidence and coordinate review. The buyer makes the final technical, legal and commercial decision.
