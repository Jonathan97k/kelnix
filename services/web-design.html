COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful details before we begin.

Have a requirement that is not listed? Send it through and we can assess it without assuming it is already part of the standard scope.

What types of websites do you build?

Business websites, service pages, landing pages, portfolios, booking or enquiry sites, marketplaces and e-commerce experiences can be scoped.

Can you connect payments?

Payment integration can be assessed for the selected provider, country, account eligibility, fees and technical requirements. No provider integration is assumed until checked.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive behaviour across common mobile, tablet and desktop sizes is part of the design and testing scope.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

Deployment and ongoing maintenance can be included or arranged as separate items depending on the project and hosting requirements.
