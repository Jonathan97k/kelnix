PROJECT QUESTIONS

Good projects start with clear expectations.

Here are a few practical points to consider before starting a website or platform project.

Ask about your requirements ↗
What information should I provide to get started?

A short description of your business, the purpose of the website, the audience you want to reach, examples you like and any required features are a useful starting point. We can help clarify the rest during discovery.

Can you redesign a website we already have?

Yes. We can review the existing site, identify the main issues and propose a redesign or targeted improvements based on your objectives and the condition of the current platform.

Can you build an online store or connect payments?

These features can be assessed as part of the project. The final approach depends on the payment provider, account eligibility, country availability, fees and technical requirements.

Do you provide hosting and ongoing maintenance?

Deployment, hosting configuration and ongoing maintenance can be discussed as part of the proposal. The recommended setup depends on the website's traffic, functionality, ownership and operating needs.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope, content readiness, integrations and review cycles. We confirm milestones after understanding the requirements rather than giving every project the same timeline.
